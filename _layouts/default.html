I am a Research Scientist at Apple Machine Learning Research, where I work on efficient on-device tool-calling agents and reasoning compression. My broader research focuses on multimodal foundation models that combine vision, audio, and language for fine-grained perception, grounded reasoning, and cross-modal generation. I am particularly interested in improving their efficiency and reliability through post-training.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Dinesh Manocha, and my MTech in Computer Science & Engineering from IIIT Hyderabad, advised by Prof. C. V. Jawahar.

During my PhD, I worked on audio-visual reasoning and alignment at Apple with Chun-Liang Li and Karren Yang; multisensory egocentric perception at Meta Reality Labs with Ruohan Gao; speech-driven facial synthesis in the Talking Heads team at Google Research with Avisek Lahiri and Vivek Kwatra; and multimodal audio generation at Adobe Research with Joseph K J. I also worked with Mohamed Elhoseiny at KAUST and collaborated with Kristen Grauman and Salman Khan.

Before my PhD, I developed video enhancement algorithms with the Camera and Video AI team at ShareChat and vision systems for consumer devices at Samsung R&D Institute Bangalore. My earlier research included work at ISI Kolkata with Ujjwal Bhattacharya and IIT Kharagpur with Pabitra Mitra.
